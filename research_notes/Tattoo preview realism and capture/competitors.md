# Competitor landscape: tattoo preview / try-on / mockup tools (2025–2026)

> Method note (read first): research was done 2026-10-02. Direct page fetches were blocked by the sandbox network proxy for apps.apple.com, play.google.com, inkjin.com, trytattoo3d.com and trustpilot.com, so **every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries of those pages, not from first-hand page reads**. Prices, ratings and counts should be treated as "as indexed by search", may be a few months old, and should be spot-checked on the live store pages before publication. Where two snippets disagree, both are given.

## 1. What the main products do (method, platform, realism, true size, pricing, ratings)

### Takeaway
The market splits into four groups: (a) phone AR camera overlays (Inkhunter, Inkjin, TattooTune, InkStudio, Tattoodo's try-on), (b) AI generators that paste or re-render a design into a client photo (Tatship, Tatspark, AI Tattoos, BlackInk AI, Fotor, InkPreview, Tat Ink), (c) 3D body previewers (Try Tattoo 3D on the web, "Tattoo Preview – 3D & AR" on iPad, Tatship/Tatspark "3D toggles"), and (d) the professional workflow of Procreate plus purchased 3D body OBJ models (Tattoo Smart, Etsy and Gumroad sellers). None that I found clearly advertises measured true-to-scale size (cm/in on a body of known dimensions); most are aimed at consumers and are paid through weekly or monthly subscriptions or credit packs.

### Inferences
- Realism ranking by method: AI photo re-render (Tatship, InkPreview, Tat Ink) gives the most "photo-real" stills but is generative, so line fidelity is not guaranteed and it is not true-to-scale. AR overlays give live movement but the design looks flat and sticker-like and jitters. 3D body tools (Try Tattoo 3D, Tattoo Preview 3D & AR, Procreate OBJ) show wrap and placement best, but on generic bodies with basic shading.
- The only true iPad-native 3D competitor found is the new "Tattoo Preview – 3D & AR" app. Its traction is unknown. Try Tattoo 3D is the closest web competitor but has negligible revenue (about $210/month), which suggests the "3D body preview" category is under-served rather than saturated.
- Weekly subscriptions ($4.99–$12.99/week) are the norm in consumer apps and are the main source of complaints. A one-time or studio-licence price would stand out.

### Gaps
- No live store data could be read: current rating counts, exact IAP lists and recent review text are unconfirmed for "Tattoo Preview 3D & AR", Inkjin, InkStudio and AI Tattoos.
- "Skin & Ink 3D" and "Ink Studio" (as a 3D app): I found no evidence that a product named "Skin & Ink 3D" exists. "Ink Studio" turned up only as the InkStudio AI generator.
- Blender and ZBrush workflows: I found no sources on artists using Blender or ZBrush for client previews (as opposed to building models for sale). Blender appears only as the tool used to create Procreate models.
- I found no source confirming that any product supports measured true-size (cm/in) on a calibrated body or photo.

## 2. Pricing models, user counts and review complaints

### Takeaway
Consumer try-on apps run on weekly or monthly subscriptions or credit packs ($4.99–$29.99), and their reviews are dominated by billing and paywall complaints, followed by loss of features, tracking and size problems, and AI quality. The professional route (Procreate $12.99 plus $3–$60 of body models) is all one-time purchases.

### Cited Findings
- Inkhunter: complaints include immediate annual charges after choosing a "free trial", removal of design upload, poor AI designs and freezing. — [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unveil-inkhunter-app-feedback-analysis-essential-insights-app-store-us-150700)
- Tatship: users like the try-on but many complain about the "paywall" and short trial. — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tatship-tattoo-design-try-on/id6746807416?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone); [Nemovideo](https://www.nemovideo.com/alternative/tatship)
- BlackInk free tier makes designs public and deletes them after 30 days, which is a privacy and ownership trade-off. — [Atlas Cloud](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/tips/best-ai-tattoo-generators)
- Size and warp: Inkhunter designs can look "ridiculously small or somewhat warped" until adjusted. Simulators are fine for placement, size and composition but do not reproduce ink spread in the dermis or aging. — [Encrevive](https://encrevive.com/en/blog/tattoo-try-on-apps/)
- Scale: the largest user base found is Tattoo my Photo 2.0 (10M+ downloads, about 306K reviews). Inkhunter has about 25K iOS ratings. Tatship has 691. TattooTune has 6. — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/tattoo-my-photo-2-0/com.mobile.bizo.tattoo.two); [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/inkhunter-ai-tattoo-design/991558368); [App Store Tatship](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tatship-tattoo-design-try-on/id6746807416); [App Store TattooTune](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tattootune-tattoo-try-on-app/id6475301980)
- No-sign-up, free and browser-based is used as a selling point (Inkjin, Tatship, Tat Ink, Tatspark, InkStudio). — [Encrevive](https://encrevive.com/en/blog/tattoo-try-on-apps/); [Inkjin](https://inkjin.com/en/try-on-tattoos-ar)

### Inferences
- Tracking jitter is a widely known AR weakness, but I could not pull specific review quotes about jitter (see Gaps). The evidence I found points more to warping and size problems and to billing.
- Privacy (uploading body photos to cloud AI services) is an implied concern given public-by-default free tiers. No source surfaced explicit privacy complaints.

### Gaps
- No direct review quotes about AR "jitter" or "drift" were retrievable, and no Reddit threads were readable. Reddit content appeared only indirectly.
- No download counts were available for Inkjin, Tatship (Android), AI Tattoos or InkStudio.

## 3. How studios show mockups today, and what they want

### Takeaway
Most artists design on iPad in Procreate. For consults they overlay the design on a photo of the client's body part (often with a Multiply layer to judge skin tone) or paint onto a purchased 3D body model. Clients increasingly arrive with AI-generated images that set unrealistic expectations.

### Cited Findings
- The "vast majority" of modern tattoo artists design on iPad with Procreate, which is called the gold standard. — [Tattoo Smart blog](https://tattoosmart.com/blogs/news/why-every-tattoo-artist-is-using-procreate)
- Common practice: put the design on a photo of the client's skin and set the layer to Multiply to preview colour on that skin and allow for yellowing as it ages. Artists show the iPad at in-person consults. Some still wrap a paper printout around the arm, but a photo is easier when fitting a design between existing tattoos. — [Envato Tuts+ Procreate for tattoos](https://design.tutsplus.com/tutorials/how-to-use-procreate-for-tattoos--cms-109190)
- 3D models in Procreate are described as a "game-changer for consults" (for example, mocking up full black-and-grey sleeves). One buyer used a kit for a lettering consult and the client signed off the same day. These are testimonial-style quotes from vendor pages, so they are biased. — [search summary of Gumroad/vendor pages](https://procreatetattoostudio.com/); [delightfuldesignstudio](https://delightfuldesignstudio.com/human-model-for-procreate/)
- AI-image requests are rising: about 1 in 4 enquiries at one Adelaide studio included AI images (ABC, Aug 2026). A Nottingham artist gets up to 10 per week. Artists say these images have errors and too much detail and cannot be reproduced on skin. — [ABC News 2026-08-06](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-06/rise-in-ai-generated-tattoo-requests/106945744); [Euronews 2026-09-09](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/09/would-you-let-ai-design-your-next-tattoo-tattoo-artists-say-ai-is-changing-the-way-they-wo); [Resultsense Oct 2025](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2025-10-29-ai-tattoo-creativity-impact/)
- Tattd (TechCrunch Startup Battlefield, Oct 2025) uses generative AI mock-ups plus reverse image search to match clients with artists, a different "preview to booking" approach. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/31/tattd-gave-four-techcrunch-writers-tattoos-at-startup-battlefield)

### Inferences
- The artist workflow is all manual. Photo overlays have no wrap or curvature, and 3D models are generic bodies that do not look like the client, have no real-world scale, and suffer edge distortion. A tool that mixes "client's own photo" with "accurate 3D wrap and true size" bridges the two current workflows.
- Because artists are pushing back on unrealistic AI images, a preview that is honest and not generative (it keeps the artist's exact linework, scale and believable healed look) fits their side of the conversation.

### Gaps
- I could not read first-hand Reddit r/TattooArtists or r/tattoos threads (fetching was blocked), so statements about what artists "wish existed" rest on vendor and blog summaries.

## 4. Gaps an iPad web tool with an accurate 3D body plus client-photo mode could fill

### Takeaway
No product found combines (1) a body that matches the client's measurements at true scale, (2) correct wrap around limbs, (3) melanin-aware skin and fresh, healed or aged ink rendering, (4) a client-photo mode, (5) on-device or offline privacy, and (6) a hand-off to sizing and quoting. Each competitor covers one or two of these.

### Cited Findings
- True size: competitors offer "resize" or "scale" controls (TattooTune, Tattoo Preview 3D & AR, Try Tattoo 3D) but advertise no cm/in measurement. — [TattooTune](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tattootune-tattoo-try-on-app/id6475301980); [Tattoo Preview 3D & AR](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tattoo-preview-3d-ar/id6760173684); [Try Tattoo 3D](https://trytattoo3d.com/)
- Body shape: Procreate packs come in fixed body types (Tattoo Smart: 4 types; Etsy: slim, athletic, curvy), not parametric bodies. — [Tattoo Smart](https://tattoosmart.com/products/model-humans-collection); [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4358402340/3d-male-body-parts-tattoo-mockup-bundle)
- Skin tone: Try Tattoo 3D offers a "custom skin tone" in its $4.99 Solo tier. Tatship claims automatic skin-tone adaptation through AI. — [Try Tattoo 3D pricing](https://trytattoo3d.com/pricing/); [Tatship](https://tatship.com/)
- Healed and aged: only AI claims exist (InkPreview "healed ink", AgeMe 50-year aging). Simulators "don't reproduce ink spreading or line aging". — [InkPreview](https://www.inkpreview.app/); [AgeMe](https://ageme.tattoo/); [Encrevive](https://encrevive.com/en/blog/tattoo-try-on-apps/)
- Wrap: Procreate 3D shows edge distortion on arms. Inkjin claims curvature-aware AR. — [Etsy review summary](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1155856741/procreate-3d-female-model-procreate); [Inkjin](https://inkjin.com/en/try-on-tattoos-ar)
- Quote integration: none of the preview tools found link a previewed size or placement to pricing. Stencil apps and generators are separate purchases. — [Encrevive iPad apps](https://encrevive.com/en/blog/top-7-ipad-tattoo-design-apps-2026/)
- Pricing pain: weekly subscriptions and trial billing are the top complaints. — [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unveil-inkhunter-app-feedback-analysis-essential-insights-app-store-us-150700); [Tatship reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tatship-tattoo-design-try-on/id6746807416?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)

### Inferences
- Positioning opportunities for a studio-facing iPad web tool:
  1. **Measured true size**: show cm/in live while resizing, on a body built from the client's height and build. No competitor clearly does this.
  2. **Parametric body plus client photo**: neither Procreate packs (fixed bodies) nor AI photo tools (no geometry) cover both.
  3. **Non-generative, artwork-faithful rendering** with fresh, healed and aged ink and melanin-based skin. This answers artists' anti-AI-hallucination stance while still looking more realistic than sticker overlays.
  4. **No install, works offline, client photos stay on the device**: the opposite of cloud AI apps with public-by-default free tiers.
  5. **One-time or studio pricing** and no weekly subscription: avoids the main complaint in reviews.
  6. **Hand-off to quoting**: pass size, placement and area to the quote (no competitor does this).
- Main competitive threats: "Tattoo Preview – 3D & AR" (same iPad 3D concept, new, traction unknown); Try Tattoo 3D (web, cheap); and Procreate plus Tattoo Smart (entrenched habit, one-time cost, artists already own it).

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether "Tattoo Preview – 3D & AR" or Try Tattoo 3D offer measurement, body-shape morphs or photo mode, because their pages could not be fetched.
- No usage or market-size data exists for studio-side preview tools.
